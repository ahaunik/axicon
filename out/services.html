Technical Inspection

Comprehensive inspection services ensuring asset integrity, regulatory compliance, and operational safety across industrial sectors.

API 510/570/653 Inspections

Pressure vessel, piping, and storage tank inspections per API standards with certified inspectors.

  • In-service & turnaround inspections
  • Fitness-for-service assessments
  • RBI & corrosion monitoring

Non-Destructive Testing (NDT)

Full suite of NDT methods performed by ASNT/ISO 9712 certified Level II & III technicians.

Welding Inspection

  • WPS/PQR qualification
  • In-process & final inspection
  • Welder qualification testing

Statutory inspections and certification of cranes, lifts, and rigging equipment per MOM/WSH regulations.

  • Periodic thorough examinations
  • Load testing & certification
  • Wire rope & chain inspection
